How it works

From binder to deal in three steps.

Scan and list

Photograph your cards. PipTCG identifies each one and adds it to your vault. List anything for sale with a price and condition.

Find and chat

Browse cards near you or wait for a wishlist alert. Message the seller, add cards to the trade box, and agree on a price.

Meet and confirm

Pick a public meetup spot in the app. Both of you confirm the handoff, ownership transfers, and you can leave a review.

Safety

Local trading, without the guesswork.

The trust signals in PipTCG are earned, never bought. Nothing you pay for changes ranking or reputation.

  • Verified meetupsPick a public spot in the app. The handoff is GPS-verified so both sides know it happened.
  • Reviews from real trades onlyYou can only review someone after a completed deal, so ratings reflect actual transactions.
  • 18 and upPipTCG is for adult collectors. Age verification is part of signup.
