Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 6, 2026

Pip ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you use the Pip mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Pip, we collect the following types of information:

Account Information: Your name, email address, username, and profile photo when you create an account.

Collection Data: Information about the trading cards you add to your collection or wishlist, including card names, images, conditions, grades, and price preferences.

Transaction Data: Details about trades you initiate or participate in, including trade offers, messages, and completion status.

Communications: Messages you send to other users, feedback submissions, and support requests.

Device Information: Device type, operating system, app version, and unique device identifiers for app functionality and troubleshooting.

Usage Data: How you interact with the app, including features used, screens viewed, and actions taken, to improve our service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To display your trading card collection and wishlist
  • To facilitate trades and transactions between users
  • To enable messaging and communication features
  • To send notifications about trade offers, messages, and app updates
  • To moderate content and enforce our community guidelines
  • To respond to your feedback, suggestions, and support requests
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve app features
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

We share your information in the following circumstances:

With Other Users: Your public profile, username, collection listings, and trade offers are visible to other Pip users. Messages are shared with the intended recipients.

With Service Providers: We use third-party services for hosting, data storage, analytics, and push notifications. These providers only access your data as needed to perform their services.

For Safety & Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or government request, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of Pip, our users, or the public.

With Your Consent: We may share information for purposes not described here with your explicit consent.

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Admin Access to Your Data

Members of the Pip team may access your account and account-related data when there is a legitimate operational need to do so. Admin access is limited to the following purposes:

Customer Support: To respond to support requests, troubleshoot issues, and recover accounts at your request.

Trust & Safety: To investigate user-submitted reports about other users, content moderation, fraud prevention, and enforcement of our community guidelines. Private conversation contents (direct messages) are only viewed when a report has been filed against that conversation — admins cannot browse private chats arbitrarily.

Legal Compliance: To comply with subpoenas, court orders, regulatory requirements, or other legal obligations.

Service Operation: To diagnose system errors, prevent abuse, and maintain service availability.

Every privileged admin action — including account bans, content removals, and conversation views — is recorded in an internal audit log so we can answer "who accessed what and when." We follow the principle of data minimization: admins only access the specific data needed for the task at hand, and access is restricted by role.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard cloud infrastructure. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure authentication and session management
  • Access controls limiting who can view your data
  • Regular security assessments

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

Access & Correction: You can view and update your profile information, collection data, and preferences at any time through the app settings.

Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us through the Help & Support section. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

Communication Preferences: You can manage your notification preferences in the app settings to control what notifications you receive.

Blocking: You can block other users to prevent them from contacting you or viewing your profile.

Data Export: You may request a complete copy of your personal data by contacting our support team. We will provide a machine-readable export within 30 days, in compliance with GDPR Article 15 (right of access) and CCPA right-to-know requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

Pip is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us through Help & Support.

8. Third-Party Services

Pip may contain links to or integrate with third-party services, including:

  • Card grading services (PSA, Beckett, CGC, Tag) for reference purposes
  • Image hosting and storage services
  • Analytics and crash reporting tools

These third-party services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies before providing them with your information. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

Service Providers

We currently work with the following service providers to operate Pip:

  • PostHog — product analytics, to understand how the app is used and improve it.
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting, to diagnose and fix problems in the app.
  • OpenAI — AI processing. Card photos you scan are processed to identify the card, and user-generated content may be processed for content moderation.
  • Apple and RevenueCat — in-app purchase processing, if premium features are enabled on your account. Payment details are handled by Apple; we do not receive your payment card information.

These providers only receive the data necessary to perform their services on our behalf.

9. Cookies & Tracking

Pip uses local storage and standard mobile app technologies to:

  • Maintain your login session
  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Analyze app performance and usage
  • Deliver push notifications

We do not use browser cookies as Pip is a mobile application. We may use anonymous analytics to understand how users interact with the app.

10. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. Specifically:

  • Account data is retained until you request deletion
  • Messages are retained for the duration of the conversation
  • Trade history is retained for record-keeping and dispute resolution
  • Feedback and reports are retained to improve our service

After account deletion, we may retain certain information as required by law or for legitimate business purposes such as fraud prevention and dispute resolution.

Fraud-Prevention Records

To keep banned users off the platform, we retain certain identifiers (email, phone number if provided, device identifiers, and recent IP addresses) associated with banned accounts after account deletion. IP address records expire after 30 days.

11. International Users

Pip operates from the United States. If you access Pip from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate.

By using Pip, you consent to the transfer of your information to the United States and other countries that may have different data protection laws than your country of residence.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will notify you of material changes by:

  • Posting a notice within the app
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Sending a push notification for significant changes

Your continued use of Pip after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through:

  • The Help & Support section in app settings
  • Submitting a suggestion or complaint through the feedback form
  • Email: sju080@gmail.com

We will respond to your inquiry within a reasonable timeframe.
